# View Switcher

**Status:** Final  
**Type:** Foundations — global, cross-view

The persistent control for moving between Fosta's five views. A floating, detached pill rather than a bar fixed flush to the bottom edge of the window.

## What it holds

The five view tabs — Desk, Sort, Develop, Track, Share. Nothing else. Capture is not part of it; Capture lives in the [App Header](app-header.md).

## Behaviour

Always visible, on every view, regardless of what else is on screen. Clicking a tab switches views immediately. The active view's tab is visually distinguished from the other four.
